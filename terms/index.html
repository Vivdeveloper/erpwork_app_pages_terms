What the app is

ERPWork is a mobile companion app for ERPNext (Frappe). It connects to an ERPNext site that you or your organisation already runs, and lets you view and work with that site's data from your phone. The app is free to download and is supported by ads (see Ads).

ERPWork is independent of ERPNext and Frappe; see About ERPNext and Frappe.

ERPNext is a free and open-source ERP (accounting, sales, purchasing, stock, CRM, projects and more), and Frappe HR adds HR and attendance. Both are built on the open-source Frappe Framework and made by Frappe Technologies with a worldwide community.

Your account and your ERPNext site

  • The app does not create accounts. You sign in with an account on an ERPNext site, which is created and managed by that site's administrator.
  • Your organisation's ERPNext site, not the developer, stores your business data and decides who can see or change it. Your use of that site is also governed by your organisation's own rules and agreements.
  • If you have the System Manager role, the app lets you turn on push notifications for your site (Settings > Notifications > Enable for this site). This adds the "ERPWork Push Token" DocType and the "ERPWork Push" Webhook to the site, and from then on the site sends its notifications' title and text through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to its users' phones. Only do this if your organisation allows it. You can disable or delete them on the site at any time.
  • If you have the System Manager and Script Manager roles, the app also lets you turn on ERPWork AI for your site (Settings > ERPWork AI > Enable for this site). This adds the "ERPWorkAi Settings" DocType and the "ERPWork AI Chat" API Server Script to the site; from then on, users who add their own AI provider and key can send questions and the ERPNext data needed to answer them from the site to that provider (see ERPWork AI). Only do this if your organisation allows it. You can disable or delete them on the site at any time.
  • You are responsible for keeping your login details safe and for activity done with your account in the app. If you turn on "remember", your login is saved on your phone; protect your phone with a screen lock, and use the app lock if others use your device.
  • The app may link to Frappe Cloud for people who don't have an ERPNext site yet. Signing up there is a separate service under Frappe Cloud's own terms.

ERPWork AI

ERPWork AI is an optional assistant in the app. It works only after it is set up on your ERPNext site and you add your own AI provider and API key ("bring your own key").

  • Your provider, your key, your costs. You choose the provider (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Google Gemini) and use your own or your organisation's account with it. Your use of the provider is governed by its own terms, usage policies and privacy policy, and any charges it makes are between you (or your organisation) and the provider. The developer does not provide, resell or control the AI service. Make sure your organisation allows sending its business data to the provider you choose.
  • What is sent. Your questions, any photo you attach and the ERPNext data the app looks up with your own permissions to answer them are sent from your ERPNext site to your provider. The Privacy Policy explains this in detail.
  • AI can be wrong. Answers are generated by AI, are labelled "AI-generated", and may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. They are not professional, financial, legal or tax advice. Check important information in ERPNext before relying on it.
  • You confirm every change. ERPWork AI can only propose creating a Lead, Opportunity, Customer, Contact, Timesheet, ToDo or Task, or changing a record's status. Nothing is saved until you review it and tap Confirm, and it is saved under your own account and permissions. You are responsible for the changes you confirm. ERPWork AI cannot read or change the site's setup (DocTypes, fields, scripts, roles, users, permissions or settings).
  • Reporting answers. If an AI answer is offensive, harmful or inappropriate, report it with the Report option on the answer, or email us at choudharyvivek195@gmail.com, so we can improve the safeguards.
  • We may change, limit or remove ERPWork AI or its supported providers at any time.
