ERPWork release notes

What's new in each version of ERPWork (Android package com.buildwithvivek.erpnext). The build number is shown in the app under Settings > About, for example "Version 1.0.0 (9)".

Version 1.1.0 Coming next

October 2026

ERPWork AI (new, optional)

  • Ask about your ERPNext data in plain words, in English, Hindi or Hinglish: "Show my overdue tasks", "Sales this month". Answers come with tappable cards for the records. Open it with the AI button on any screen.
  • Create Leads, Opportunities, Customers, Contacts, Timesheets, To Dos and Tasks, or change a status, by asking. Nothing is saved until you check it and tap Confirm.
  • Attach a photo, for example a business card, to create a Lead from it.
  • Bring your own key: each user picks Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Gemini and adds their own API key in Settings > ERPWork AI. The key is saved on your ERPNext site and never shown again. A System Manager turns it on for the site with one tap.
  • It looks things up with your own permissions, and it can't read or change the site's setup. Chats are saved only on your phone (your last 30). Answers are AI-generated and can be wrong.

Point of Sale

  • Sell from your phone with your POS Profile: open and close the shift, scan or search items, add a new customer, take payments and make returns.
  • Creates POS Invoices, or Sales Invoices on ERPNext v16 when POS Settings say so.
  • Receipts in your print format, with Share PDF. Past sales in POS history.

HR and approvals

  • New HR dashboard: today's attendance, leave, expense claims, holidays and pending approvals (HR staff see everyone, others their own).
  • Approvals inbox: documents waiting for your workflow action, and Leave Applications and Expense Claims where you are the approver. Approve or reject in one tap.

Work offline, stay in sync

  • Saved data stays readable offline. Comments, status changes with a remark, follow-up To Dos and check-ins made offline are sent when you're back online; see and retry them in Pending changes.
  • A Live / Auto / Offline pill on Home shows whether the app is in sync. Tap it, or pull down on a screen, to sync now.
  • Scan barcodes to find items.
  • Create Customers, Quotations and Sales Orders in the app.

Privacy and more

  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked on screens with business data (dashboards, lists, documents, reports, POS and ERPWork AI).
  • Crash reports help us fix problems faster. They carry no ERPNext data.
  • Share ERPWork with colleagues, and rate it, from Settings > About.

Version 1.0.0 (build 9) Coming next

October 2026

Push notifications

  • Alerts for assignments, mentions, approvals and your site's notification rules now arrive even when ERPWork is closed. Tapping one opens the document.
  • Works for every site you are signed in to. A notification from another site switches the app to that site and opens the document there.
  • One-tap setup: a user with the System Manager role turns it on for the whole site in Settings > Notifications > Enable for this site. Other users need no role. Everyone else can send the setup steps to a System Manager from the same screen.

Sites and account

  • Stay signed in to up to five sites and switch between them without signing in again.
  • Profile: switch site or company, and change your password.
  • Privacy policy and terms open inside the app.

Dashboards

  • New Manufacturing, Accounts and Assets dashboards.
  • Period filter on every dashboard: Today, Week, Month, Quarter, Year or Custom.
  • Company filter at the top of Home and every dashboard. It starts on your ERPNext default company and applies to everything that has a company.

Lists and documents

  • Create Leads, Opportunities, To Dos and Tasks in the app (the + button, and on the My To Do and My Tasks cards), and edit records with the new Edit button. Forms follow your site's own fields, including custom fields.
  • Edit Leads and Opportunities right on the record, in the same design.
  • Change the status of a To Do or Task by tapping its status.
  • Assign and Remind me on every list and document you have access to.
  • New card design for every list, status tabs with counts, progress for Work Orders and Job Cards, and stock per warehouse on Items.
  • Search, filter and sort in one compact row.

Improvements

  • Better fit on phones with large text or a notch; the status bar follows the app theme.
  • Permissions are asked with your phone's own dialogs only.
  • Simpler screens: Cancel only on CRM records, no Duplicate, no Region & company screen, and no profile photo or bell on the Home header.
  • "Powered by ERPNext" on the sign-in and About screens.

Ads

  • ERPWork is free and shows small banner ads marked "Ad": one fixed at the bottom of Home, dashboards, lists, documents and the ERPNext tab's list pages, and one in the middle of Home and dashboards. None while editing, on forms, sign-in or settings, and none during the first day after install.
